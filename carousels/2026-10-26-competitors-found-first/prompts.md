## 01-cover

```text
EXACT ASPECT RATIO: 3:4 vertical portrait only [1080x1440].
BACKGROUND: one full-bleed photograph with a real camera look: a single
directional light, deep soft shadows, a muted desaturated grade, fine film grain.
Not glossy 3D, not a stock-photo smile, no faces looking at the camera.
PALETTE: the photograph stays muted near-black, charcoal and concrete grey.
Type in off-white #F4F1EA. Exactly one accent: signal orange #FF5A1F. No other
bright colours anywhere.
TYPOGRAPHY: a huge heavy tight sans headline taking most of the width, with
normal spaces between words. Exactly one phrase per slide switches to orange or
to an italic display serif. Body in a light clean sans, small. Render every
quoted string exactly, letter for letter, with perfect spelling, and add no
other words anywhere.
SIGNATURE: every photograph shows one subject singled out from many identical
ones or from a large empty space, marked by one loose hand-drawn signal-orange
marker circle. Type may overlap the photo but never covers the singled-out subject.
FOOTER: handle "@teamloopfound" bottom left in small off-white sans, a thin
off-white arrow bottom right, on every slide except the last.
NEVER: page numbers, dots, Instagram interface, like or comment counters,
watermarks, real company logos, placeholder text, prices, statistics.

Slide type: cover, the hook that stops the thumb.

Small off-white pill at the top left: "Swipe. Three fixes."
Upper third: a small line in light sans, "Your website isn't the problem." Directly under it the hero, massive heavy sans almost the full width, in signal orange: "Nobody can find it."

Photograph: a high aerial view of a dark city street at dusk lined with many identical shopfronts, all unlit. One small shop in the lower middle has its lights on and its door open. Light comes only from that one shop window, spilling warm onto the wet pavement. Everything else in deep shadow. No people's faces, no readable signs. The lit shop is circled once with a loose orange marker loop.

Composition: headline in the upper third over the dark rooftops; the circled shop stays clear of all type.
Consistency: slide 1 of six, same grade, type, accent and footer as the cover.
```
