Most owners ask for reviews the wrong way.

"Leave us a review" usually gets you a star and two words. That tells a new customer almost nothing, and AI search is reading the words, not only the stars.

Three small changes:
1. Ask what you did: "What did we help you with?" gets a real answer.
2. Ask the same week, while the job is still fresh. Recent reviews count.
3. Reply in plain words and name the service. It shows you're active.

Never write reviews for customers or pay for them. Just ask better.
