## What the strong ones share (01, 02, 05, 06)
1. A full-bleed photograph with a muted or dark grade and one clear light source.
2. One subject that stands apart from a crowd or a big empty space.
3. A huge heavy sans headline; one phrase switched to an accent colour or italic serif.
4. Exactly one bright accent colour against neutrals.
5. Very few words: 5 to 20.
6. A tiny footer line and an arrow, nothing else.

## What the weaker ones do (avoid)
- ref_04: every line the same loud weight, text filling the whole frame, a large logo.
- ref_02 / ref_04: real brand logos on the slide.
- ref_01: a fake like/comment counter (Instagram interface).

## The idea that fits LoopFound
Every reference that worked shows **one thing found among many**. That is exactly our promise: get found.
Our motif: one subject singled out in each photo, marked with a hand-drawn signal-orange circle.
