## Keyword automation + second-AI rules (Lesson 9)

```text
Write two files for my account (see CLAUDE.md).
1. automations/found.json: keyword "found". Five public replies, none with a
   link or a site name, each under 12 words. A first DM in plain text that
   asks for the word "send". Require follow: true. The delivery: a title, two
   sentences, [LINK], a button label. One follow-up 90 minutes later. Ask for
   email: true.
2. dm-rules.md: everything the second AI knows. Who we are in two lines, the
   voice (one to three short lines), never invent, when to answer SKIP, never
   message first, stop when a human has replied.
Never invent a link. If you need one, ask me for it before writing anything.
```

Test from a second account that does not follow you: public reply (no link) → plain DM in requests → follow check after "send" → card with a working link.

## Weekly review (Lesson 9, stage 10)

```text
Attached: this week's funnel table (one row per carousel) and CLAUDE.md.
1. Work out the two rates for each post.
2. Rank the hook types (contrarian, number, doing it wrong) by average
   keyword comments, and state how many posts each ranking rests on.
   Anything under three posts is a hint, not a finding.
3. Tell me honestly what seven posts can and cannot show.
4. Give me exactly one change for next week, and one thing to keep the same.
5. Write next week's brief: five topics, none repeated from this week, a hook
   type for each, and the one change at the top.
```
Rates: delivered ÷ keyword comments, emails ÷ delivered.
